# ROCKNIXDS 1.6.4.3-plus for the RG DS Plus (agiacalone fork)

The DraStic engine moves to SuperDrastic **0.5.0-beta.1-rocknixds.11**: fixes brought over from the BananaOS line
of SuperDrastic, built and tested for ROCKNIX.

- **No long white screen after a sleep.** After a sleep of about 36 minutes or more, DraStic could show white
  panels and hang for up to another 36 minutes. A gap of more than a second in its clock is now taken out
  ([BananaLinux #64](https://github.com/JorreFog/BananaLinux/issues/64)).
- **21 more shaders** in the in-game menu's shader list: 11 from libretro (LCD grids, CRT, scanlines, FXAA and
  more) and 10 converted from Anbernic's DraStic and NNDDSS. All 29 compile on ROCKNIX's Mali driver; this fork
  makes libretro's parameter lines acceptable to it.
- **ds-grid-2x is 13% cheaper** on the GPU, with the same picture, and ds-grid's grid is thin and even on the RG DS.
- **Pop-ups for DraStic's save and load state buttons:** "Saved to slot n" once the state is written, "Loaded slot n"
  once it is read.
- **A charger shows as charging** in the in-game menu ([#35](https://github.com/JorreFog/BananaLinux/issues/35)).
- An opt-in sound rate for display-locked pacing (`DSFLIP_AUDIO_LOCK_PER`), off unless set.

Everything in 1.6.4.2-plus stays. The engine's source is
`dsflip/superdrastic-0.5.0-beta.1-rocknixds.11.patch` (11 commits on SuperDrastic `d8f4105`); `SUPERDRASTIC` says how
it was built and checked.

Update from the menu (*Updates & downloads > ROCKNIXDS*), or install over ssh on a handheld running ROCKNIX:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/agiacalone/ROCKNIXDS/main/install.sh | sh
```
